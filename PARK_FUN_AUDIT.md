# Park Fun and Purpose Audit

## Approval test

No component is approved merely because it is detailed, themed, or fills blank
land. Each must have a player verb, a visible outcome, a replay reason or
declared one-time purpose, a spatial job, an operational plan, and a justified
block/entity cost.

| Class | Minimum contract |
|---|---|
| Experience | Ride, solve, compete, collect, or choose; visible outcome; entry, exit, reset, and service access. |
| Recovery | Seating, utility, social moment, or a route payoff; reachable from an exit without blocking flow. |
| Orientation | A real decision-point view, map, or threshold pointing to walkable anchors. |
| Scenic support | Frames a view, supports a ride, conceals service, protects a void, or marks a boundary. Never called an attraction. |

If a proposal passes none of these, remove it.

## Revised program

| Former proposal | Decision |
|---|---|
| Fireworks Terrace | Remove. A passive event deck is not a reliable gameplay loop. |
| Midway Food Hall | Reduce to one Snack Window, 4-6 seats, and route-integrated exit vista. |
| Hall-scale Frontier Saloon | Reduce to compact social counter, 6-10 seats, and one small interaction. |
| Standalone galleries/outlooks | Retain only as payoff on an existing exit/return route. |
| Passive Hollow Parlour | Make it a small social/clue room with direct return, no food service. |
| Hollow reveal chamber | Build only as an optional challenge with a puzzle and outcome. |
| Themed façade rows | Prohibit unless a façade contains an interior, counter, queue, service edge, boundary, or interaction. |

| Land | Actual fun core | Supporting verbs |
|---|---|---|
| Midway | Carousel, Sky Lift, 3-4 distinct games | choose, compete, collect, regroup, depart |
| Frontier | Mine Coaster, 2-3 Prospecting games | ride, watch, aim, time, collect, return |
| Hollow | Mirror Maze, Manor clue route, Ossuary puzzle | explore, solve, choose, discover, socialize |

Hollow deliberately differs from the ride lands. It specializes in information
gameplay: partial clues, route choice, secret discovery, timed state changes,
and shared puzzle outcomes. Puzzles are short, legible, resettable, optional,
and never lock a required public route.

## Isthmus: two journey chapters, not a sculpture garden

The current Isthmus is structurally valid but programmatically overfull:
39,903 blocks over two 101-block gaps, with four unrelated creatures, pool,
garden, and overlook. The Pale Wyrm/Python is a detached roadside object:
it has no player verb, outcome, route role, or operational need.

Connectors are not a fourth land. Their job is transition, traversal, and at
most one optional micro-experience per reach. The 5-wide main path remains fast
and continuous; optional features leave it and reconnect ahead.

### Frontier Reach: The Claim Line

| Piece | Job | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Existing narrow causeway + skyway | Transit/spectacle | Preserve safe crossing, lighting, and rail-pier reserve. |
| One mine signal or ore-cart marker | Orientation | Marks Frontier transition and frames Mine Ridge. No creature sculpture. |
| Prospect Pull-out | Micro-game | One 20-45 second resettable target/timing interaction with lamp/bell result, in a side alcove. |
| Geological edge/void view | Scenic support | Small rock/moss/trestle rim treatment makes bridge credible and protects the edge. |

Remove the standalone pool, garden roundel, duplicate seating stops, and both
large creature installations on this reach unless one later gains a specific
structural/story role.

### Hollow Reach: The Wyrm's Crossing

Rebuild the Python/Wyrm as a threshold mechanism rather than a statue:

1. A dark-stone/bone serpentine rib or head arches beside the optional alcove,
   visibly tethered to bridge rim and Hollow approach.
2. Three player-height rune inputs have discoverable clues from the head,
   route plaques, and Mirror Maze/Manor story.
3. Correct sequence opens a 2-wide optional shortcut to the return path and
   changes an obvious local light/state panel; wrong input cleanly resets.
4. Normal transit bypasses it. Reward is discovery or acknowledgement, never
   economy-critical loot.
5. Reset/service redstone is behind the rim/head and reachable from the
   protected side; no floating coil or snake-on-grass silhouette remains.

If that compact mechanic is not server-safe, remove the Wyrm entirely and use
an articulated Hollow rib/gate boundary. Do not retain a purposeless snake.
The balloon may remain only as one Sky Lift wayfinding icon; Heron and Sloth
retire from the connector unless later assigned a concrete story/route role.

## Generator requirement

Every public config must carry a fun contract: class, player verbs, visible
outcome, replay or one-time purpose, required interfaces, and where applicable
reset, service access, and a main-route bypass. An experience lacking those
facts fails generation; scenic support needs a declared spatial job.
