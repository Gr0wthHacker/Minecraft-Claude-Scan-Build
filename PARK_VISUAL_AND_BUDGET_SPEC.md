# Park Visual Direction, Setpiece Ledger, and Block Budget

This document controls visual density and material spending for the final park. It exists to prevent two opposite failures: thin, underbuilt generators that look like prototypes, and expensive bulk palette use that gives no player-facing value.

## Target scale

The completed park has a target construction budget of **265,000 blocks**, excluding temporary scaffolding and any server-managed entities/items. This is a target range, not a quota: a module may use fewer blocks only when its silhouette, player interfaces, and visual packet still meet this specification.

| Program | Target blocks | Visual job |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| shared paths, retaining edges, service, lighting, safety | 42,000 | makes the island feel intentionally built and safe rather than objects on void |
| Midway, Arrival, Carousel, Sky Lift, games, Sloth | 50,000 | brightest social/arrival composition |
| Frontier, Mine Ridge, Coaster, Prospecting, Heron | 66,000 | dense working-expedition silhouette and ride terrain |
| Prismworks, Spire, Array, Vault, Forge, service core | 78,000 | high-detail vertical machine landmark and night identity |
| Claim Line, Wyrm’s Crossing, attached supports/rim treatment | 18,000 | credible transitions, not extra lands |
| final detail reserve, visual corrections, night/safety pass | 11,000 | detail after human review, never generic filler |
| **Total** | **265,000** | **full destination scale** |

## Material policy

| Tier | Target share | Use freely / sparingly |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| cheap structural/detail | 78-86% | stone, cobblestone, stone-brick family, dirt/moss, logs/planks, fences, slabs/stairs, leaves/vines, wool, water, legal low-cost lights |
| okay accents | 10-16% | glass panes, iron bars, selected metal-like functional trim, small sign/rail/support details |
| expensive functional exception | 2-5% | only declared rails, target blocks, redstone lamps, comparators/observers, and server-payment interfaces where they have a proven role |
| expensive decorative bulk | 0-1% | terracotta, concrete, quartz, glass blocks, prismarine, purpur, copper are prohibited as mass fill; use only after a named visual exception |

A wall does not become premium because it is made from a costly block. Spend block count on depth, shadows, support rhythm, openings, profiles, terrain layering, cables, frames, feather courses, and roof/crown construction first.

## Visual hierarchy

1. **Prism Spire** is the right-side crown and night beacon.
2. **Mine Ridge and Coaster** form the left-side kinetic silhouette.
3. **Sky Lift** is Midway’s tall central invitation.
4. **Signal Heron, Sky Lift Sloth, and Wyrm’s Crossing** are the only creature/setpiece moments.
5. Everything else frames routes, queues, working buildings, or these anchors.

No new standalone animal, monument, topiary field, balloon collection, or void sculpture may be added without retiring an existing callout. The park needs memorable images, not more objects.

## Landmark construction density

Every hero build must have three readable distances.

| Distance | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 50+ blocks | unmistakable silhouette, one dominant gesture, contrast against sky/void |
| 15-35 blocks | visible structural rhythm: layers, supports, arches, ribs, track, wing courses, frames, or roof profile |
| 1-12 blocks | craft detail: proper block orientation, trim depth, signage, accessible rails, texture variation with purpose, no unsupported filler |

For buildings, use a minimum three-depth façade grammar: foreground posts/sign/rail, recessed wall/window/door layer, and roof/back mass. For terrain, use base mass, mid ledge/retaining layer, and sparse surface accent. For rides, every track/cable/water line must have an adjacent support or operating reason.

## Required visual packets

Human review is mandatory at these views before detail is promoted:

- park arrival facing Midway;
- Frontier approach and Mine Ridge profile;
- Midway court facing Sky Lift and Sloth;
- Prism Reach facing Foundry Gate and Wyrm;
- Foundry Gate facing Spire;
- Prism Spire at day, night, launch level, catch level, and Forge Deck;
- Heron, Sloth, and Wyrm at intended approach and close range;
- one overhead composition view showing only the three high crowns.

Reject a view if a landmark has no readable silhouette, one module blocks another’s approach view, a creature looks detached, or bright accents flatten the hierarchy.

## Budget release rule

The first 180,000 blocks should establish massing, paths, supports, terrain, usable interiors, queues, exits, and night-safe lighting. The remaining budget is released only after the first full visual packet identifies exactly where added density improves a named view, support story, player interface, or animal anatomy.

Do not spend the reserve on random lamps, scattered vegetation, expensive colour bands, or duplicate props.
