# PF Vantage Midway Belvedere - archived 2026-09-03

Jack, standing on its crown: "there is a large building/tower right next to the air balloon
design... its squished between the balloon and the railway."

He was right. The tower's own docstring already admitted the squeeze: 15x15 at V139/U228, its
west door opening straight onto the Circus ring at V138 because there was nowhere else on that
lot to put it, and its east face eighteen columns short of the railway at V172. A vantage that
has to touch both landmarks to fit is not sited, it is wedged.

Kept whole so nothing is lost: the design (litematic + sidecar + render) and its config, exactly
as shipped. Not tracked in `sync.yaml`; does not regenerate. To bring it back, copy the config
into `configs/`, re-run `python -m mcbuild gen configs/pf_vantage_midway_belvedere.yaml --ship`,
and add `"PF Vantage Midway Belvedere"` back to `EXTRAS_READY` in `tools/park_place.py`.

`PF Claim Lake Range` (configs/claim_lake_range.yaml) replaces it in the same reach - sited on
the exit/observation band's own open lawn, off Claim Lake's east shore, well clear of both the
Circus and the railway.
