## Executive decision

Retire Hollow. The right-hand land becomes **Prismworks: The Skyfoundry**:
a bright, strange, vertical Skyblock discovery district for mastery and
cooperative play. It is neither a haunted street nor another food/retail row.

Player sequence: arrive, see Prism Spire, choose free discovery loop or clearly
priced premium challenge, score/complete, claim proof, watch others, choose
another challenge, return.

Mirror Maze is no longer horror. Rebuild its useful geometry as Prism Array, a
colour-and-signal navigation challenge, or retire it if it cannot provide clear
route logic and a real outcome.

There is one food offer in the entire park: Midway's small Snack Window.
Frontier and Prismworks have rest/water/seating only; no food halls, restaurants,
decorative market stalls, or vendor rows.

## Grass economy

Grass may buy a real, optional run. It never pays for park entry, paths, exits,
viewpoints, maps, essential puzzles, or basic access. It is not chance/gambling.

| Spend | Player receives | Mandatory proof |
|---|---|---|
| Trial entry | one defined scored challenge attempt | approved payment adapter, visible price, confirmation, safe failed-payment/refund state |
| Replay | another attempt at published rules | reset and anti-stall timeout |
| Cosmetic/show unlock | non-random named acknowledgement | no advantage plus reset/expiry rule |

Prices stay in configuration. Before any paid module ships, confirm Skyblock.net
payment rules. Every paid experience has a free observer/demo view and free
public bypass.

## Midway and Frontier

Midway remains the compact arrival/play hub: admission, Welcome Court, Carousel,
Big Wheel/Sky Lift, three or four mechanically distinct games, a compact prize
point, one Snack Window with 4-6 seats, and Frontier/Prism thresholds.

Frontier remains the high-energy mine expedition: Trailhead, visible Mine Ridge,
Mine Coaster station/ridge/return, two or three distinct Prospecting games,
Assay & Prize Office, compact Saloon Counter with 6-10 seats, Mining Square,
and concealed Works Yard. It has no restaurant.

## Prismworks: right-hand Skyfoundry

### Land promise

Prismworks is an impossible workshop around a vertical energy/prism machine.
Colour and motion advertise challenges; every activity reports an understandable
result. Solo competition and small-group cooperation make it a lingering land.

Spatial sequence: Prism Reach → Foundry Gate → Calibration Court → Prism
Concourse. From the concourse, guests choose Prism Array, Prism Ascent,
Resonance Vault, or Forge Deck, then return. The 5-wide concourse always
bypasses queues. Challenges use separate 2-3-wide queues, 3-wide exits, and a
concealed reset/control path.

| ID | Component | Class | Player value / contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | Foundry Gate + Calibration Court | orientation | map, challenge board, visible Spire, rules/demo wall, rest/water point, never paid entry |
| P02 | Prism Spire | landmark/infrastructure | carries signals, lights, and vertical route interfaces; accessible base control room and safety boundary |
| P03 | Prism Array | free skill challenge | Mirror Maze rework: choose valid colour/signal gates, reach finish, show time/state, reset from rear; real wrong routes, no softlock |
| P04 | Prism Ascent | high-intensity visible parkour | free practice route plus optional timed premium run: checkpoints, safe fall catch, result board, observer paths, normal bypass; no forced PvP |
| P05 | Resonance Vault | cooperative puzzle | 2-4 separated roles, visible state changes, shared completion; solo-safe fallback or clear minimum-player status |
| P06 | Forge Deck | social/observer | compact scoreboard, achievement banners, 6-10 rails/seats, challenge selector on return route |
| P07 | Archive of Attempts | reward | non-random completion wall and milestones; not lootboxes or shop |
| P08 | Service Gallery | operations | protected reset, wiring, payment, storage, and safety reach behind all challenges |
| P09 | Aether gardens / prism rockwork | scenic support | sparse transition, boundary concealment, and material contrast; no decorative maze |

Why it lingers: Array is free and repeatable by routing/time; Prism Ascent is
visibly exciting to observers; Vault creates cooperation; achievement display proves
mastery; players can watch, learn, attempt free mode, and only then choose a
clearly valuable premium run.

Vertical plan: service/Vault core at B-48 through B-8; gate, Array, Vault
entries, exits and observer deck at B-8 through B+28; Spire/Prism Ascent platforms
at B+28 through B+80; one restrained crown above only when view corridor allows.

## Full Isthmus and connections

The rail viaduct remains separate infrastructure. The walking causeway is two
journey chapters, not a fourth land.

Shared rules: retain 5-wide spine centered at X=97595; preserve the rail-pier
exclusion east of X=97640; maintain safe rim/lighting/void protection; allow
one optional off-spine feature per 101-block reach; keep walking, rail, and
service separate; never charge for passage.

### Frontier Reach: Claim Line

Frontier Gate, trailhead marker, narrow causeway, then Midway Arch. One mine
signal/ore-cart marker frames Mine Ridge. A side Prospect Pull-out is built only
if it provides a 20-45 second resettable timing/target loop distinct from the
Prospecting Porch; otherwise it is a protected void-view transition. Remove
pool, garden, repeated benches, Heron, and Sloth.

### Prism Reach: Wyrm's Crossing

Midway Arch, bright-to-prismatic transition, then Foundry Gate. Python/Wyrm is
rebuilt as an optional threshold mechanism:

1. Dark-stone/bone rib/head physically ties to bridge rim and Foundry approach.
   No detached snake-on-grass shape.
2. Three player-height rune inputs have clues from connector and Prism Array.
3. Correct sequence changes an obvious local state and opens a 2-wide optional
   shortcut/rejoin; wrong input resets cleanly.
4. Main path bypasses it. Any grass purchase is an explicitly-described
   optional challenge mode, never passage or shortcut.
5. Reset, safety, and payment components are behind rim/head on service side.
   If the mechanic is not server-safe, delete Wyrm and use a simple Prism rib/gate.

Balloon may remain once as Midway Sky Lift wayfinding, never as a connector
stop. No other creature sculpture survives without route, structural, or
playable purpose.

## Build order

1. Freeze WorldSpec: rename Hollow plots/anchors to Prismworks, reserve Spire
   and challenge volumes, declare Isthmus edges.
2. Greybox spines, queues, exits, observer positions, service paths, and
   vertical safety volumes. Reject before visual detail.
3. Generate Midway/Frontier anchors and Prism Array trial geometry.
4. Add mechanics/payment only after bypasses, reset paths, and service pass.
5. Build Wyrm Riddle as standalone connector module; test all states.
6. Add compact support, landscape, signage, night pass, then run route,
   mechanics, safety, cost, transaction, and visual evidence gates.
