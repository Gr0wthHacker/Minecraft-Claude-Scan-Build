# Animal Setpiece Release Standard

Animals are hero setpieces, never background filler. A finished animal must be recognisable from its intended player view, reward close inspection, and remain a single physically buildable assembly after every finishing step.

Use an enforced `animal_contract` for every public animal config. It requires a minimum block and material budget, one 6-connected component, a declared best review view, and named anatomy actually reported by the generator. An enforced contract also requires `design.visual_review: true`, which emits the four-angle packet and silhouette for human approval.

Automatic checks reject placement problems, disconnected limbs, flat low-material sculptures, undeclared anatomy, and functional/mechanical blocks used as animal skin. They do not replace the final human question: can a player name the creature from the intended approach and does its face/pose look deliberate? Record that review before promotion.

For the park, use only animals with a spatial job:

- Signal Heron: tall Frontier visual beacon and approach landmark.
- Hanging Sloth: a close-view underside discovery, with branch, mask, face, limbs, and claws.
- Any later animal must earn its place through a sightline, destination, or route moment; do not add decorative duplicates.
