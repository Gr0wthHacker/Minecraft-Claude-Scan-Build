# 600 by 200 Park Offering Audit

## Decision standard

This audit judges an offering as a Skyblock.net destination, not as a screenshot.
A strong offering has a clear verb, visible outcome, replay or social value,
spectator value, honest grass-spend potential, manageable operations, and a
meaningful vertical/footprint role.

Scores use 0-3 per criterion: verb, outcome, replay, spectator, vertical value,
and operational simplicity. A score is evidence for comparison, never a taste
verdict. An offering with no player verb or outcome fails regardless of score.

## 600 by 200 planning envelope

Treat 600 by 200 as a finite program envelope, not a demand to cover 120,000
cells in buildings. The following is a normalized plan along the long axis U
and depth axis V. Map U/V onto real world axes only after the WorldSpec locks
connector edges and the existing island capture is reconciled.

| U band | Width | Program | Area |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| 0-169 | 170 | Frontier: mine town, ridge, coaster, compact games | 34,000 |
| 170-214 | 45 | Frontier Reach: Claim Line connector | 9,000 |
| 215-384 | 170 | Midway: admission, family rides, compact games, dispatch | 34,000 |
| 385-429 | 45 | Prism Reach: Wyrm Crossing connector | 9,000 |
| 430-599 | 170 | Prismworks: Spire, Array, Vault, observation/return | 34,000 |

Within every land, V is measured from the public/connector edge toward the
protected outer edge:

| V band | Depth | Allocation rule |
|---|---:|---|
| 0-23 | 24 | threshold, visible landmark cue, arrival orientation |
| 24-127 | 104 | primary playable floor, queues, exits, and public loops |
| 128-151 | 24 | recovery/reward and route-integrated observation only |
| 152-169 | 18 | concealed service spine, reset access, operational rooms |
| 170-199 | 30 | protected rim, terrain, support roots, view/void reserve |

The envelope is fully used by program, circulation, service, safety, terrain,
and views. It is not fully occupied by blocks. A land with 20-30 percent
intentional public openness is healthier than a dense theme facade field.

## Vertical allocation

Assume the current park build plane B near 202 and Java range -64 through 335.
These bands reserve the full available height without treating all of it as
buildable mass.

| Band | Relative range | Primary allocation | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crown | B+80 to B+133 | Big Wheel crown, Mine Ridge peak, Prism Spire crown | only three skyline dominants; preserve view corridors |
| Attraction high | B+28 to B+80 | coaster lift/ridge, Sky Lift gallery, Prism Ascent route | player movement/structure only; no unrelated towers |
| Public surface | B-8 to B+28 | entries, games, queues, exits, courts, Prism Array | most activity; clear routes and sightlines |
| Hidden core | B-48 to B-8 | service, catches, machine cores, compact cooperative puzzle rooms | no generic basement filler |
| Deep adventure | B-160 to B-48 | one optional climax route only, if it has challenge/outcome/return | reserve by default; do not duplicate Prismwork's main loop |
| Void reserve | -64 to B-160 | rare finale or structural void | leave empty unless a specific safe finale proves value |

## Zone composition and vertical use

### Midway

Surface owns admission, Welcome Court, Carousel Court, three/four games, prize
point, one Snack Window, and departures. Sky Lift rises into attraction-high
band and exits back into the surface return. Midway has no deep route, food hall,
or competing crown. Its open court is needed for choice and social visibility.

### Frontier

Surface owns Trailhead, Main Street, Mining Square, compact Prospecting Porch,
Assay, and return. Attraction-high band is Mine Ridge and Coaster lift/trestle.
Hidden core is purely dispatch/service and optional ride scene transition.
Deep adventure is unreserved until a water/rail expedition has proven distinct
from Coaster. Mine Ridge, not another tower, consumes the skyline budget.

### Prismworks

Surface owns Calibration Court, Prism Array, Vault entries/exits, challenge
selection, and bypass. Attraction-high band is Prism Ascent around the Spire,
with bubble launch rising from surface to high deck and parkour descending to
Forge Deck. Hidden core contains catches, service, and Vault logic. Deep space
stays a reserve until the surface challenges are repeatably fun; it must never
be used to pad a parkour descent. Crown is one Spire cap only.

### Connectors

Both 45-wide reaches use surface-level safe transit and deliberate palette
transition. Their below-grade space is service/support only. They must not add
new crowns or deep dungeons. Frontier Reach has at most one optional micro-game.
Prism Reach has Wyrm Riddle only if its three-input mechanism passes and a
normal bypass remains.

## Gaps that still need real work

1. Prism Array and Prism Ascent are plans, not built generators. They need
   separate bounded-Spire modules, greyboxes, contracts, and block tests.
2. No approved grass payment adapter exists. Until server behavior is verified,
   every run is free and no config claims payment.
3. The current parkour generator is a full-island descent. Prism Ascent needs a
   new generator that owns a Spire plot, launch shaft, catch volumes, service
   accesses, checkpoints, and observer paths.
4. Current Isthmus is a 39,903-block passive sculpture program. It must be
   replaced by the two-reach plan before any new connector details are added.
5. Water rides remain unproven. Do not spend the large footprint or Skyblock
   materials until a real in-game rider test validates them.
6. Existing output artifacts and old Hollow configs are historical, not
   authority. Prismworks WorldSpec, routes, and interfaces must replace them.

## Promotion decision

Build now: admission/paths, Carousel, Sky Lift, Mine Coaster, three or four
best games, Prismworks greybox, Prism Ascent practice geometry, Prism Array,
and compact connector greyboxes.

Build only after proof: timed/paid runs, Resonance Vault, Wyrm Riddle,
Prospecting micro-game, water ride, persistent score/leaderboard, any deep
adventure layer.

Do not build: Hollow assets, fireworks terrace, food court, chance casino
games, duplicate rail rides, disconnected statues, decorative market/retail,
or any paid route/essential interaction.
